Signature therapies

Classical treatments, done the traditional way.

A glimpse of the therapies practised at Sun Matha. Each is selected and sequenced by our physicians to match your constitution and condition.

  1. 01Abhyangamഅഭ്യംഗംFull body A synchronised, full-body warm oil massage that improves circulation, eases muscular tension and calms the nervous system — the foundation of most Panchakarma programmes.
  2. 02KizhiകിഴിPotli massage Warm herbal potlis (poultices) are pressed rhythmically over the body to relieve joint and muscle pain, reduce stiffness, and improve mobility.
  3. 03ShirodharaശിരോധാരHead & mind A continuous, gentle stream of warm medicated oil flows over the forehead — deeply calming for migraines, stress, anxiety and disturbed sleep.
  4. 04Nasyamനസ്യംNasal therapy Medicated nasal drops that clear the head and sinus passages — effective for sinusitis, migraines and chronic congestion.
  5. 05Kativastiകടിവസ്തിSpinal care Warm medicated oil is pooled over the lower back inside a dam of black gram paste — a targeted therapy for chronic back pain and lumbar conditions.
  6. 06PichuപിചുDeep therapy Warm medicated oil-soaked cotton is applied to affected joints or the spine for sustained, deep relief in chronic pain conditions.
