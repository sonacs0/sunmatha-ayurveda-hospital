Questions

Before you visit.

Answers to what patients ask us most. Can't find yours? Call +91 93532 68528.

How many days should I plan to stay?

It depends on your condition. Our physician will suggest the right length of stay after your consultation.

Some chronic conditions need longer — one patient with long-standing hand pain followed a 45-day programme.

Do I need a consultation before treatment?

Yes. Every treatment plan begins with a consultation, so the therapies can be chosen and sequenced for your condition and constitution. Call or WhatsApp us to arrange one.

Can varicose veins be treated without surgery?

Non-surgical care for varicose veins is the treatment Sun Matha is best known for. Whether it is right for you depends on your condition, so the physician will assess you first and explain what to expect.

Will a female patient be treated by a female therapist?

Always. Female patients are treated exclusively by female therapists, in separate treatment rooms set aside for their sessions.

What food is served during the stay?

Simple, organic vegetarian meals made from home-grown produce, planned to support the therapies you are receiving.

How do I find the hospital in Kalpetta?

We are at Affas Apartment, opposite the New Bus Stand, Kalpetta — easy to reach by bus or taxi from anywhere in Wayanad. Call us when you arrive and we'll guide you in.

What if I'm not happy with my treatment?

We offer a 100% satisfaction promise. If you're not satisfied with your care, tell us and we will make it right.
