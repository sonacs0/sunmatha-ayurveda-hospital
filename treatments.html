01Full-body oil massage

Abhyangam

അഭ്യംഗം

A synchronised, full-body massage performed with warm herbal oils chosen for your constitution. Two therapists work in rhythm to improve circulation, ease muscular tension, and calm the nervous system. Abhyangam is the foundation most Panchakarma programmes are built on.

Traditionally used forGeneral fatigue, muscular stiffness, stress, and as a preparatory therapy before deeper treatments.
02Ayurvedic bandaging

Upanaham

ഉപനാഹം

A herbal paste is applied to the affected joint and held in place with a warm bandage over an extended period, allowing the medicine to work continuously into the tissue.

Traditionally used forChronic joint pain, arthritis-type stiffness, and swelling.
03Deep oil therapy

Pichu

പിചു

Warm medicated oil-soaked cotton is placed over an affected joint or along the spine for a sustained period, allowing deep, concentrated absorption into the tissue.

Traditionally used forChronic joint and spinal conditions, localized deep-seated pain.
04Lower-back therapy

Kativasti

കടിവസ്തി

A dam made of black gram paste is built around the lower back, then filled with warm medicated oil that is kept pooled over the area for a set duration — a precise, targeted therapy for the lumbar spine.

Traditionally used forChronic lower back pain, lumbar spondylosis, disc-related discomfort.
05Herbal poultice

Kizhi (Potli Massage)

കിഴി

Warm herbal potlis — cloth poultices packed with heated herbs, rice or leaves — are pressed rhythmically over the body. The combination of heat, herbs and pressure eases stiffness and improves mobility.

Traditionally used forJoint pain, muscular stiffness, sports-related strain, arthritis-type conditions.
06Blood-letting therapy

Rakthamokshanam

രക്തമോക്ഷണം

A classical purification therapy performed under strict clinical supervision to relieve localized inflammation and toxin build-up, used selectively for specific chronic skin and circulatory conditions.

Traditionally used forCertain chronic skin conditions and localized circulatory concerns, including varicose vein care.
07Head massage

Siro Abhyangam

ശിരോഭ്യംഗം

A focused scalp and head massage with warm herbal oils, releasing tension held in the head, neck and shoulders while nourishing the scalp.

Traditionally used forTension headaches, hair fall, dandruff, stress and poor sleep.
08Nasal drops

Nasyam

നസ്യം

Medicated oil or herbal drops are administered through the nostrils to clear the head and sinus passages, addressing conditions that begin above the shoulders.

Traditionally used forSinusitis, chronic congestion, migraines, and certain ENT conditions.
09Herbal steam bath

Swedanam

സ്വേദനം

A herbal steam bath, usually following an oil massage, that opens the pores and channels to help the body release toxins and absorb the benefit of the preceding therapy more fully.

Traditionally used forStiffness, poor circulation, and as a complement to Abhyangam and Kizhi.
10Foot massage

Pada Abhyangam

പാദാഭ്യംഗം

A dedicated warm-oil foot massage targeting pressure points and circulation in the feet and lower legs — deeply relaxing and often used to close out a day of treatment.

Traditionally used forLeg fatigue, poor circulation, general relaxation, and disturbed sleep.
11Oil-stream therapy

Shirodhara

ശിരോധാര

A continuous, gentle stream of warm medicated oil is poured over the forehead in a steady rhythm for a fixed duration. It is one of the most requested therapies at Sun Matha for its deeply calming effect.

Traditionally used forMigraines, anxiety, stress disorders, vertigo, and insomnia.
12Hair & scalp care

Lakshmi Theru Hair Oil

Our own herbal hair oil formulation, applied and massaged into the scalp, part of our approach to hair and scalp conditions alongside Siro Abhyangam.

Traditionally used forHair fall, dandruff, dry scalp, and premature greying.
